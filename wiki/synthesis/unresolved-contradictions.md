# Unresolved Contradictions

## Contradiction 1 — Retail footprint

**Public (Board Meeting Transcript, October 15, 2025):**
> "We are fully committed to expanding our physical footprint in the Northeast corridor by 15% next year."
> — David Joyner, FY2026 strategic outlook

**Internal (VP Retail Ops Slack summary, November 20, 2025):**

> — Prem Shah's direct report, in #retail-ops-leadership

**PwC implication:** Any pitch materials citing the 15% expansion are stale. The retail rationalization opportunity is larger than originally scoped — likely $20M+ in operations consulting if positioned before Q2 earnings.

## Contradiction 2 — AI savings timeline

**PwC implication:** The 9-month slip is the wedge for our data architecture pitch. Aetna cannot hit the public AI savings target without first solving the data foundation — which is exactly the $45M Total Patient Experience opportunity. Recommend immediate consultation with Tilak Mandadi before Q1 Steering Committee.

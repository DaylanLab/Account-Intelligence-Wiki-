# CMMC L3 Readiness — Federal Defense Contractor

## Opportunity Summary

Two-year CMMC Level 3 attestation program for a top-10 defense contractor with $4B+ in DoD revenue at risk. Scope: NIST SP 800-172 controls implementation, third-party assessor (C3PAO) coordination, and a continuous monitoring overlay.

## Why Now

The client's largest DoD contract renewal window opens Q3 2027 and requires L3 attestation. The 18-month assessment cycle means the program must launch by Q3 2026.

## Estimated Value

$18M across 24 months. PwC retains 60%, federal practice retains 40% under the joint pursuit agreement.

## Competitive Landscape

Deloitte has the incumbent CMMC L2 program. We need to demonstrate L3 differentiation — likely through the continuous controls assurance platform we're piloting with the F100 insurer.

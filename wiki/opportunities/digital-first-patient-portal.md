# Digital-First Patient Portal 2.0

## Opportunity Summary

Replatform the existing CVS patient portal into a holistic health-management hub, integrating prescriptions, primary-care scheduling (Oak Street + MinuteClinic), benefits lookup (Aetna), and AI-driven adherence nudges.

## Why Now

Joyner's WSJ Health Summit keynote (Feb 10) named "the patient front door" as a 2026 priority. Mandadi's team has internal budget but lacks the integration design capability.

## Projected ROI

220% over a 30-month horizon, weighted heavily toward Year 2 once prescription-adherence economics kick in.

## Risks

Competing internal proposal from the Aetna digital team to extend the Aetna app instead. Recommend positioning as patient-centric (CVS Pharmacy-led) before that proposal gains traction.

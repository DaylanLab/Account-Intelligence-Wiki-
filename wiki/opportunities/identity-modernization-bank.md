# Identity Modernization — F500 Northeast Bank

## Opportunity Summary

Three-year migration from legacy CA SiteMinder to a hybrid Okta + Microsoft Entra identity platform. Workstreams: directory consolidation, privileged access modernization, customer IAM uplift, and DORA-aligned third-party access controls.

## Why Now

DORA enforcement (January 2026) made the third-party access gap an audit finding in the client's most recent regulatory exam. Board-level mandate to close the gap within 36 months.

## Estimated Value

$32M over 36 months, weighted to Year 1 (directory consolidation + governance) and Year 2 (privileged access).

## Competitive Landscape

McKinsey has been pitching a Palo Alto-centric architecture leveraging the new HashiCorp alliance. Our differentiator: Microsoft + Okta hybrid with PwC controls overlay, validated against DORA Article 28.

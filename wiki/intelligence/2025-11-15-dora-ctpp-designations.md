# DORA Critical Third-Party Provider Designations

## Summary

In November 2025 the EU's competent authorities designated 19 ICT service providers — including AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, and other major hyperscalers — as Critical Third-Party Providers (CTPPs) under the Digital Operational Resilience Act. CTPPs are now subject to direct EU supervisory oversight, with authority to request specific security measures, remediate vulnerabilities, and impose administrative (and in some cases criminal) penalties for non-compliance.

DORA itself entered application on January 17, 2025.

## Why It Matters

Pre-CTPP-designation, DORA put compliance pressure on the financial entities themselves. Post-designation, the hyperscalers are also on the hook directly, which changes contract dynamics, shared-responsibility-model interpretation, and the upstream evidence chain that financial-services clients depend on.
